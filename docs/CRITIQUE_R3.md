## Summary

NO REFERENCE WAS AVAILABLE. refs/ contains only its README.md — zero reference captures. I did NOT perform the blind A/B the rubric describes and I am not claiming one. Every score below is against docs/VISUAL_RUBRIC.md plus my own knowledge of how shipped air-combat titles (War Thunder, IL-2, Ace Combat 7, and the cel-shaded reference points Guilty Gear Xrd / Ni no Kuni / Genshin) actually look on screen. All 10 frames were captured fresh via `node tools/shoot.mjs --out shots/round3 --warmup 9000` and every PNG in /Users/paulius/Dev/cel-thunder/shots/round3/ was opened and inspected. Verdict: 0 of 10 frames pass. Every single frame has at least one axis at or below 4, and eight of ten trip an explicit automatic-failure clause in the rubric. The build has genuine strengths — the HUD data panels (POWERPLANT/AIRFRAME) are the work of someone who has actually looked at a shipped flight HUD, the hedgerow field system in dogfight.png is a real environment idea, and the cloud deck in clouds.png has volume. But those strengths are sitting on top of three systemic failures that a player notices in the first ten seconds: the aircraft are not lit, they cast no shadow, and the VFX are placeholder. Specifically: (1) Every aircraft in all ten frames is flat-lit. There is no terminator, no sun-driven rim, no self-shadowing under the wing, no value difference between a surface facing the sun and one facing away. In sunset.png the aircraft is backlit by an enormous sun and has zero rim light — that is the single most expensive missed frame in the set. (2) Not one aircraft casts a shadow on the ground, including ground_attack.png and low.png where the aircraft is low over open fields. src/render/passes/ShadowRig.ts contains an elaborate, well-reasoned fitted-shadow implementation whose output is not visible anywhere; combined with RenderSystem.ts:176 `shadowMap.autoUpdate = false` and RenderSystem.ts:383 `shadowMap.needsUpdate = false`, the map is almost certainly built once at a stale camera pose and never re-fitted, so subjects fall outside the fitted sphere. A 300-line comment block explaining crisp aircraft shadows, and no shadows on screen. (3) The propeller disc, src/assets/aircraft/build.ts:549 — `opacity: 0.34, depthWrite: false, bands: 2` — renders as a flat grey-black translucent ellipse in eight of ten frames. It is not a motion smear; it is a tinted oval that visibly darkens the sky, terrain and clouds behind it. In sunset.png it punches a large grey hole in the best sky in the build. This is the most-repeated single defect in the set. Beyond those: ground_attack.png is showing an outright rendering bug — large hard-edged translucent grey quads with horizontal scanline striping and what look like garbled glyph shapes are laid over the terrain across the entire lower half of the frame, most likely a cloud-shadow or decal pass sampling the wrong atlas region. That frame cannot ship in any form. cockpit.png is not a cockpit: there is no instrument panel, no gunsight glass, no interior sidewall, no pilot, no stick — just bare untextured canopy tubing over a flat dithered ochre nose that fills 60% of the frame. Both HUD frames carry the "SERVER UNAVAILABLE — FLYING OFFLINE" banner (the WebSocket failure the harness logged) plus faint leftover debug labels ("IAS 4??", "MAP0 A 44", "0/2 ... 10") baked at low contrast behind the instruments — the rubric calls unstyled debug overlay an automatic failure and it is present in both. The damage VFX are cartoon soap-bubble circles for smoke and an opaque red-orange plastic blob for fire, with no light spill onto the airframe. Vegetation across every terrain frame is the same three tree stamps at identical scale with identical pure-black outlines at all distances, another explicit automatic failure. Nothing here is close. This is a competent renderer with an art direction that has not been finished.

## Highest-leverage changes

1. SINGLE HIGHEST LEVERAGE — Light the aircraft. Every one of the ten frames scores 3-4 on Lighting for the same reason: the in-world cel material is not receiving a usable sun term, so there is no terminator, no sun-driven rim, and no self-shadowing on any airframe. src/ui/menu/HangarViewer.ts:73-77 already builds a correct key (0xfff0d8) / fill (0x9fc6ff) / rim (0xffd08a) rig for the hangar; the in-world path does not have an equivalent. Drive the cel band offset from N·L against the actual scene sun vector in src/render/CelMaterial.ts, add a hard sun-coloured rim term keyed to sun azimuth (with the terminator warmed and the shadow band tinted toward the sky colour rather than grey), and enable self-shadow reception on the hull. This one change lifts Lighting, Material, Silhouette and 'Is this AAA' across all ten frames simultaneously, and it is the difference between sunset.png being a throwaway and being the marketing shot.

2. Make aircraft cast shadows on the ground. Zero of ten frames show one, including two low-altitude passes where the shadow is the primary altitude cue a player reads. src/render/passes/ShadowRig.ts contains a carefully reasoned fitted-sphere implementation that is producing nothing visible; suspect RenderSystem.ts:176 shadowMap.autoUpdate=false combined with RenderSystem.ts:383 needsUpdate=false leaving the map built at a stale pose so subjects fall outside the fitted region. Verify the rig re-fits per capture and that aircraft meshes have castShadow set and terrain has receiveShadow set.

3. Rebuild the propeller disc. src/assets/aircraft/build.ts:549-552 renders it as a flat 34%-opacity double-sided quad, which produces a grey ellipse that darkens sky, cloud and terrain behind it in eight of ten frames — the most-repeated defect in the set. Replace the uniform alpha with a radial ramp: near-zero alpha at the hub, a bright hot arc near the tip, angular streak variation, and additive rather than multiplicative blending against bright backgrounds.

4. Fix the terrain overlay corruption in ground_attack.png before anything else cosmetic. Hard-edged translucent grey quads with horizontal scanline striping and glyph-like garbage are being drawn over the lower half of the terrain, and faint traces of the same artifact appear in damage.png and hud.png. This is a live rendering bug, not an art problem — trace whatever projects a texture onto the terrain in src/world/terrainMaterial.ts and confirm it is not sampling a UI or font atlas.

5. Replace the damage VFX wholesale. src/vfx/DamageFx.ts is producing hard-edged opaque circles for smoke (soap bubbles) and an opaque static red-orange mass for fire (melted plastic). Rebuild smoke as stepped, graphically-shaded puffs with per-particle rotation, silhouette break-up, density falloff and proper dissipation, anchored to the emitter and advected by the velocity vector; rebuild fire as stepped flame licks streaming aft with sparks and embers, and add warm light spill from the fire onto the airframe skin and onto nearby terrain and cloud.

6. Depth-scale the world ink pass and fix vegetation. Tree and field-boundary outlines are pure black at uniform weight from foreground to horizon in every terrain frame — an explicit rubric automatic failure — and the trees themselves are three stamps at one fixed scale, so trees at the horizon render the same on-screen size as trees at the camera. createOutlineMaterial (src/render/CelMaterial.ts:429) already depth-scales for hulls; apply the same to the vegetation and terrain ink, tint the ink toward the ambient rather than pure black, and give src/world/Vegetation.ts at least four silhouette variants with real scale jitter, canopy clumping and perspective-correct LOD.

7.
